# SCH-85 Finance Summary Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute inline in the current `codex/sch-85-finance` branch. Keep the linked Linear acceptance criteria as the scope authority.

**Goal:** Give a solo service provider an honest monthly snapshot of money collected, money expected from upcoming appointments, and a small set of appointment and client activity metrics.

**Architecture:** Keep finance math in a pure mobile feature module. Add one workspace-protected payment amount field and RPC to the appointment model, return it with the existing appointment list, and calculate the monthly snapshot from a range-limited data set. A completed appointment can record an amount paid; the finance summary never treats a service price as a payment.

**Tech Stack:** React Native / Expo, Supabase PostgreSQL migrations and RPCs, generated TypeScript database types, i18n (RU/CZ/EN), Vitest, repository Biome checks.

---

## Product decision and competitor evidence

Research checked on 2026-10-14:

- Masters Pro's official salon statistics overview groups the home view into income, appointments, expenses, customers, and efficiency widgets. Its income widget is month-based and separates a dashboard summary from deeper reports: <https://faq.masterspro.app/article/676160a539ae110d2fabc9b9>.
- The user's Masters Pro screenshots show the same useful scan pattern: one income card with fact, forecast, and expenses; a separate appointment card; then optional clients and efficiency sections. We will keep the scan pattern and omit report-heavy sections.
- Altegio describes a broader finance module built from payment operations, cash registers, expense categories, commissions, and refunds. Its P&L uses recorded income and expenses; that is beyond this small summary: <https://alteg.io/ru/support/knowledge-base/291094/>.
- Current Schedule Masters schema stores appointment status and service price snapshots but has no payment, expense, cash register, refund, or transaction model. Therefore only the payment amount will be captured in this scope; profit will not be displayed.

## Functions shown to the user

1. Month selector with previous/next month navigation.
2. **Received:** sum of amounts the master manually records as paid on completed appointments, grouped by currency. This is not inferred from appointment price.
3. **Expected:** sum of listed prices for future pending and confirmed appointments in the selected month, grouped by currency. Past pending/confirmed appointments, cancelled appointments, and no-shows are excluded.
4. Appointment counts: completed, upcoming, cancelled (customer/master together), and no-show, each appointment counted once.
5. Client counts among completed appointments in the selected month: new means the customer's first completed appointment in workspace history is in that month; returning means at least one earlier completed appointment exists. Counts are unique customers.
6. Booked hours: duration of completed appointments plus future pending/confirmed appointments in the selected month. This is booked time, not utilization percentage.
7. Payment entry in the completed appointment details: editable total paid amount, allowing zero, partial payment, or full service price; amount is bounded by the appointment's price and currency.
8. Loading, retry/error, empty-month, demo, localization, and narrow-screen states. Copy states that this screen does not calculate profit because expenses are not tracked.

## Explicit non-goals

Expenses, profit/P&L, cash registers, payment methods, payment history/reconciliation, refunds, taxes, inventory, exports, service/customer profitability, and utilization percentage.

## Definition of done

- Received totals are sourced from manually recorded payment amounts, never from appointment price or completed status alone.
- Expected totals include only future pending/confirmed appointments in the selected workspace-local month.
- All status/client/hour metrics follow the definitions above and are covered by tests.
- The screen remains a monthly summary and does not call its totals profit.
